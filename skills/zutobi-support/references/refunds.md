# Operator-approved refund replies

Read this file only after the operator explicitly approves a refund offer for the current thread. That approval unlocks refund language, not unverified facts.

Before choosing a template, determine the purchase route from Joel or a trusted system message. Ask Joel when the route is unknown. Do not guess from the customer's device alone.

## Website purchase, offer approved

Use when Joel approves an exception but has not said that the refund was processed.

```text
Hi {name},

We can make an exception and offer a refund for the most recent payment. Please make sure the subscription is canceled, then reply to confirm. This article explains the cancellation steps:
https://zutobi.com/us/faq/managing-my-subscription-trial-and-billing

Once you confirm, we can proceed with the refund.

Best regards,
Joel
```

## Website purchase, refund completed

Fact requirement: Joel must state that the refund was processed before this template may be used.

```text
Hi {name},

I can confirm that the refund for the most recent payment has been processed. The time it takes to appear in your account depends on your payment provider.

Please also make sure the subscription is canceled so there are no further charges:
https://zutobi.com/us/faq/managing-my-subscription-trial-and-billing

Best regards,
Joel
```

## Apple App Store purchase

```text
Hi {name},

Apple handles payments and refunds for subscriptions purchased through the App Store. You can submit a refund request directly to Apple here:
https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT204084

Please also make sure the subscription is canceled so there are no further charges:
https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT202039

Best regards,
Joel
```

## Google Play purchase

```text
Hi {name},

Google handles payments and refunds for subscriptions purchased through Google Play. Follow Google's refund and cancellation instructions here:
https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/7205930

Best regards,
Joel
```

For an unauthorized-charge thread, use the ordinary category 7 template until the account and purchase route are verified. Operator approval alone does not establish either fact.
